What this is: ApocalyptiClock is an independent, unofficial project. Once an hour, an AI model checks real, recent news headlines (via GDELT) for one risk category at a time — nuclear, conflict, climate, biosecurity, AI risk, or human factors (misinformation, mass panic, negligence) — rotating through all six over a 6-hour span, and judges whether that category's risk moved up, down, or held steady, citing the specific stories behind its call. This is not the official Doomsday Clock and is not affiliated with the Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists. Treat it as one model's reading of the news, not a scientific measurement.
