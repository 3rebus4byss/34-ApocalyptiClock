What this is: ApocalyptiClock is an independent, unofficial project. Every 4 hours, an AI model checks real, recent news headlines for one risk category at a time — nuclear, conflict, climate, biosecurity, AI risk, or human factors (misinformation, mass panic, negligence) — rotating through all six over a 24-hour span, and judges whether that category's risk moved up, down, or held steady, citing the specific stories behind its call. This is not the official Doomsday Clock and is not affiliated with the Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists. Treat it as one model's reading of the news, not a scientific measurement. Use the filters below to include or exclude categories and see how the setting shifts.
How this actually works (not random)

Each check pulls real headlines from named editorial sources — BBC, Al Jazeera, The Guardian, and NPR — filtered to that category's topic. If none of those have anything relevant, it falls back to GDELT Cloud, a structured news-event database, before giving up for that check.

The model isn't judging headlines in isolation. Each check is given the category's current standing and its last few results, and is explicitly told: if the new headlines are just continuing coverage of something already accounted for, hold steady — don't re-flag the same ongoing story every few hours. The number only moves for a genuine new development, cited with a real source link you can check yourself.
